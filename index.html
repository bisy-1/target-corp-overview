Target Corporation

Ticker: TGT (NYSE)  ·  Sector: Consumer Discretionary  ·  Headquarters: Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA

Company Overview

Target Corporation is a leading American general-merchandise retailer offering a curated mix of fashionable, trend-forward merchandise and everyday essentials at discounted prices, delivered through a seamless omnichannel experience. Its stated purpose is to help all families discover the joy of everyday life.

The company operates nearly 2,000 stores across the United States, supported by a national supply-chain network that doubles as a fulfillment backbone for its digital business. Stores fulfill approximately 97% of total merchandise sales, making the physical footprint both a retail channel and a logistics asset.

Business Model

Target monetizes through five primary revenue streams:

  • Merchandise sales — owned brands, national brands, and exclusive designer collections.
  • Roundel advertising — Target's retail-media network, monetizing guest data through on-site and off-site ad placements.
  • Target Circle Card — proprietary credit-card profit-sharing with issuing partners.
  • Target Plus marketplace — third-party seller platform that complements owned assortment.
  • Target Circle 360 membership — paid subscription tier with unlimited same-day delivery, free shipping, and extended returns.

Customer Journey

  1. Awareness — discovery of stylish, affordable products via digital and in-store channels.
  2. Consideration — evaluation of value (price, owned-brand quality, designer exclusivity) and convenience (fulfillment speed).
  3. Purchase — checkout via Target.com, the Target app, or in-store.
  4. Fulfillment — same-day delivery, Drive Up, in-store pickup, or shipped from a distribution center.
  5. Loyalty — Target Circle (free), Target Circle Card (5% discount, free shipping), Target Circle 360 (paid subscription).

Strategic Priorities

Target's forward strategy emphasizes owned-brand authority (the "Tarzhay" positioning), loyalty ecosystem depth, retail-media monetization through Roundel, and same-day fulfillment as a competitive moat against pure-play e-commerce. Management has publicly stated that digital penetration (currently ~20% of merchandise sales) and Circle 360 subscriber growth are the two primary long-term value drivers.
